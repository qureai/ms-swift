\begin{abstract}
A jointly trained chest X-ray vision--language model (Qwen3.5-2B with an ML-Decoder
classification head) exhibited a large and systematic gap between its
\emph{discriminative} ability and its \emph{generative} behaviour: teacher-forced
presence log-probabilities achieved a nodule ROC-AUC of 0.830 --- statistically
indistinguishable from the dedicated 132-tag classification head (0.836) --- yet
free-running greedy generation asserted \texttt{presence:\,true} for only 13.2\% of
ground-truth nodule cases. The model \emph{knew}, but would not \emph{say}. We describe
a third supervised fine-tuning stage (S3) whose explicit objective was to move the
greedy operating point of the generated classification JSON onto the Youden-optimal
point of the model's own underlying score --- the point the ML-Decoder head reaches
when thresholded at sensitivity{\,=\,}specificity. S3 combines (i) a 403{,}820-conversation
multiturn dataset composed from 19 task-chain templates over previously trained
capabilities, (ii) a frozen vision tower and frozen classification head to preserve the
validated discriminative substrate, and (iii) an asymmetric $6{:}1$ cross-entropy weight
on the \texttt{true}/\texttt{false} presence tokens, implemented at zero cost inside the
fused Liger kernel. On 17{,}880 production studies, free-running nodule-family
sensitivity rose from 0.292 to 0.588 at specificity 0.931; the Youden index rose
$+83\%$ (0.284$\to$0.519), reaching parity with the head's equal-error operating point
on several findings (calcification $J{=}0.534$ vs.\ head $0.534$; atelectasis 0.437
vs.\ 0.448) while preserving the head's ROC-AUC exactly (frozen weights, bit-identical).
Macro Youden across 194 findings improved $+48\%$. We argue that single-point Youden
analysis --- not AUC --- is the correct lens for evaluating generative classifiers, and
derive why a $6{:}1$ token weight approximates the Youden threshold at the prevalence
regime of this dataset.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Structured-report generation and multi-label classification are usually treated as
separate problems. Our production CXR system trains them \emph{jointly}: a 2.7B-parameter
Qwen3.5-2B VLM generates a detailed classification JSON (per-finding
\texttt{presence}/\texttt{size}/\texttt{location}/\texttt{characteristics} over a
$\sim$112-finding ontology), Answer-Set-Programming (ASP) reasoning traces, knowledge
graphs, and full radiology reports, while a 12.8M-parameter ML-Decoder head attached to
the frozen-at-inference vision tower produces 132 calibrated tag scores from the same
visual features.

Evaluated on 136{,}639 production studies with report-derived silver labels, the two
faces of the same model told contradictory stories:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Discriminatively}, the model was strong. The ML-Decoder head reached a
macro ROC-AUC of \textbf{0.885} (nodules 0.836, granuloma 0.930, pneumothorax 0.970).
Teacher-forced presence log-probabilities of the LLM itself --- reading
$p(\texttt{true})$ at each finding's value slot --- yielded a nodule AUC of
\textbf{0.830}, tied with the dedicated head, and within 0.01--0.06 of it on most
parenchymal findings.
\item \textbf{Generatively}, the model was almost mute. Under greedy decoding the
free-running classification JSON asserted nodules with sensitivity \textbf{0.132}
(family-level 0.292), pneumonia 0.185, pulmonary edema 0.152, rib fractures 0.152 ---
at specificities of 0.99+. Macro sensitivity over the shared vocabulary was 0.164.
\end{itemize}

The information was demonstrably present in the network's token distribution; greedy
argmax simply never crossed the lexical decision boundary. This paper documents the
third training stage (S3) built to close that gap, its measured effect, and the
operating-point theory that motivated both its design and its evaluation.

\section{The Operating-Point Problem}

\subsection{Greedy generation is a fixed, accidental threshold}

Let $m(x) = \log p(\texttt{true}\mid x) - \log p(\texttt{false}\mid x)$ be the logit
margin at the presence-value slot for a finding in study $x$. Teacher-forced AUC
evaluates the \emph{ranking} induced by $m$ over studies --- it integrates over all
thresholds and is therefore threshold-free. Greedy generation, by contrast, emits
\texttt{true} iff $m(x) > 0$: it is a \emph{single} operating point, fixed at margin
zero by the token prior the model absorbed during training.

Two properties of the pre-S3 training data pinned that threshold in a pathological
place. First, the classification-JSON targets are overwhelmingly negative ($\sim$6 true
among $\sim$112 findings per study), so the unconditional prior of the \texttt{true}
token at any given slot is $\sim$5\%: the model must overcome a $\sim$3-nat prior
deficit before \emph{asserting} a finding. Second, symmetric cross-entropy makes every
token error equally costly, so the loss-minimising behaviour at ambiguous slots is to
emit the majority token. The result is exactly what we measured: a classifier with AUC
0.83 operating at the extreme-specificity corner of its own ROC curve (sens 0.13 /
spec 0.996 for nodules).

\subsection{Why Youden's $J$, not AUC, is the right lens}

For a \emph{generated} binary decision there is no score to sweep: ROC-AUC is
undefined. The two familiar summaries of a single operating point are balanced accuracy
$\mathrm{bAcc}=(\mathrm{Se}+\mathrm{Sp})/2$ and Youden's index
$J=\mathrm{Se}+\mathrm{Sp}-1=2\,\mathrm{bAcc}-1$. $J$ has three properties that make it
the correct target here:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{It is chance-corrected and threshold-honest.} Any trivial threshold slide
along the chance diagonal leaves $J{=}0$; sensitivity bought one-for-one with
specificity does not move it. A rise in $J$ certifies that the operating point moved
\emph{toward the ROC curve's shoulder}, i.e.\ that usable separation was gained.
\item \textbf{Its maximum over thresholds is a property of the score, not the
threshold:} $J^{*}=\max_{\tau} \big(\mathrm{Se}(\tau)+\mathrm{Sp}(\tau)-1\big)$ is the
Youden-optimal point of the underlying ROC. For the ML-Decoder head we can compute
$J^{*}$ directly (its equal-error-rate point closely approximates it); for the LLM's
teacher-forced log-probability score, the same construction applies. \textbf{These two
$J^{*}$ values are the ceiling that any decoding strategy can reach without changing
the model's knowledge.}
\item \textbf{It composes into the deployment question.} A generative report is only
usable if what it \emph{asserts} sits at a clinically sensible point; $J$ measures
precisely the quality of the assertion rule.
\end{enumerate}

This framing yields the quantitative goal of S3, stated before training began:
\emph{move the greedy operating point of the generated JSON to
$J_{\mathrm{greedy}} \approx J^{*}$, where $J^{*}$ is the maximum Youden index of the
model's own discriminative scores --- equivalently, of the ML-Decoder head (AUC 0.885)
and the LLM's log-prob AUC (0.83 nodules), which are empirically the same signal.}
Since the head thresholded at its EER point realises $J$ within 1--2\% of its $J^{*}$,
the head's EER row in our tables serves as the concrete, measurable target.

\section{Stage-3 Training Strategy}

\subsection{Substrate: what S1 and S2 built}

S1 (13.3M prompts over 4.8M conversations) taught nine task families --- sectioned and
structured report generation (9 sub-tasks $\times$ 1.06M), single- and multi-image
bounding-box grounding (2.3M), ASP reasoning (0.87M), cross-image report comparison
(0.49M), and focused COPD/nodule/device analyses. S2 added the full-ontology
classification JSON with \emph{explicit negatives} (crucially: earlier data contained
zero \texttt{presence:\,false} fields, making the presence token a syntactic constant)
and jointly trained the ML-Decoder head with an asymmetric-loss (ASL) objective on 132
tags, reaching head macro AUC 0.885 with no loss of generative ability.

\subsection{Data: 19-template multiturn composition (403{,}820 conversations)}

S3's dataset was \emph{composed}, not collected: every conversation chains existing
capabilities on the \emph{same image}, joined across the S1/S2 sources by image key.
Eight single-image chains start with the classification JSON and append grounding,
ASP, knowledge-graph, ontology-report, report-section, or follow-up turns
(templates A--H); six more end in the \emph{structured JSON report} with two
non-image-derivable sections (\emph{Identification and Administrative Data},
\emph{Comparison with Previous Images}) removed from every target (N--S); two novel
\emph{region-identify} templates invert grounding (given 1--3 box coordinates, name the
abnormality; T,U); and three multi-image templates cover per-image classification
followed by report comparison, plus bbox-comparison and nodule-bbox replay (I,K,M).
Images are allocated \emph{disjointly} across templates; 100 rows of every template form
a stratified validation set (1{,}900 rows). Two data repairs were applied during
composition: sub-100\,px$^2$ degenerate boxes were dropped, and the
\texttt{atelactasis} spelling variant (234k instances) was normalised in box labels.

The rationale: chained turns teach the model that the classification JSON, the ASP
trace, the graph, and the report are \emph{views of one committed read} --- the report
turn is trained to condition on an \emph{actually generated} classification rather than
on nothing --- while template diversity prevents any single chain from becoming a rote
sequence.

\subsection{Freezing the discriminative substrate}

The vision tower (306M parameters) and the ML-Decoder head (12.8M) were \textbf{frozen};
only the LLM (2.395B) and the vision--language projector (20M) trained
(88.3\% of parameters). The classification-head loss was disabled
(\textsc{asl\_weight}=0), as a frozen head admits no gradient path. Freezing serves the
paper's central logic: \emph{the discriminative optimum $J^{*}$ must remain a fixed
target while the generative policy moves toward it.} Any drift of the visual features
would change both the head's AUC and the LLM's log-prob AUC, making
before/after Youden comparisons unattributable. Operationally, the head's 23 tensors
are re-loaded from the S2 checkpoint at attach time (they would otherwise be silently
re-initialised) and are carried bit-identically into every saved checkpoint, so
downstream head-based inference is unchanged. A corollary requirement: geometric image
augmentation (elastic/grid distortion, 10--15\% crop-zoom) would silently corrupt
box-coordinate supervision, so augmentation is suppressed for any conversation
containing \texttt{bbox\_2d} ($\sim$53\% of rows) and retained photometrically-and-geometrically for the rest.

\subsection{Asymmetric token weighting: engineering the threshold shift}

The decisive intervention is a class-conditional weight on the AR cross-entropy,
implemented through the fused Liger kernel's \texttt{ce\_weight} vector (gathered by
target-token id; zero memory and zero throughput cost, in contrast to any
\texttt{loss\_scale} path that would materialise a $\sim$10\,GB logits tensor):
\[
w(\texttt{true})=6,\qquad w(\texttt{false})=1,\qquad w(\text{JSON scaffolding})=0.5,
\qquad w(\text{other})=1 .
\]
Consider the token-level decision in isolation. Weighted cross-entropy at a presence
slot is minimised by asserting \texttt{true} whenever
\[
w_t\, p^{*}(\texttt{true}\mid x) > w_f\, p^{*}(\texttt{false}\mid x)
\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
p^{*}(\texttt{true}\mid x) > \frac{1}{1+w_t/w_f} = \tfrac{1}{7}\approx 0.143,
\]
i.e.\ training pressure moves the greedy boundary from $p^{*}{=}0.5$ to
$p^{*}{\approx}0.143$ --- a $\approx$1.8-nat shift of the decision margin. For a
well-ranked score under class prevalence $\pi$, the Youden-optimal threshold sits near
the point where the weighted densities cross; with the 5--10\% prevalence typical of
our mid-frequency findings and the empirical score distributions of the S2 model, the
EER/Youden point corresponds to accepting positives down to roughly
$p^{*}\!\sim\!0.1$--$0.2$ --- which is precisely the region the $6{:}1$ weight targets.
The weight is thus not a heuristic ``recall boost'' but a calculated re-anchoring of
the generative threshold onto $J^{*}$. (S2 had deliberately trained these tokens at
$1{:}1$; its greedy point sat at margin~0, explaining the whole pathology of
Section~2.1.)

\subsection{Optimisation}

One epoch over 401{,}920 conversations; effective batch 1{,}024 (8$\times$H200-class
GPUs, per-device batch 8, gradient accumulation 16); LLM LR $4\times10^{-6}$, projector
$5\times10^{-5}$; bf16, ZeRO-2, Liger fused CE, gradient checkpointing, no packing
(image$\to$row correspondence must survive for the multiturn/box supervision);
393 optimiser steps, 11.7\,h wall-clock. Eval loss on the stratified set fell
0.128$\to$0.124 and plateaued from step $\sim$250; eval token accuracy was flat at
95.9\% throughout --- foreshadowing that the improvement lives in a handful of decision
tokens, invisible to token-level accuracy but decisive for $J$.

\subsection{Why the gain is so large}

The magnitude of the improvement --- triple-digit relative $J$ gains on ten findings ---
follows directly from the mechanism. S2's greedy point sat at the margin-0 corner of a
\emph{good} ROC curve: the steepest part of the curve, where a small threshold
relaxation buys sensitivity almost for free. The 1.8-nat boundary shift engineered by
the $6{:}1$ weight traverses exactly that steep region. Findings whose S2 sensitivity
was near zero but whose log-prob AUC was high (pulmonary vascular congestion: sens
0.084, head AUC 0.833) gained the most (+474\% $J$), because the discriminative signal
was fully formed and purely \emph{gated} by the token prior. Findings already near
their optimum (effusion, devices) gained little, exactly as the theory predicts.
Three secondary contributors: (i) chained-conversation training forces the assertion
set to be \emph{used} downstream (ASP, report), penalising silent omissions;
(ii) explicit-negative targets from S2 remain in the mixture, so the model does not
degenerate into over-assertion --- specificity stayed at 0.93--0.98 on most findings;
(iii) the frozen substrate means all improvement is attributable to the
policy/verbaliser, not to representation drift.

\subsection{Verification of substrate integrity and format compliance}

The frozen head is bit-identical (restored 23/23 tensors; no optimiser group), so head
AUC remains 0.885 by construction --- verified by re-scoring. Structured-report chains
generated by the S3 checkpoint are 100/100 valid JSON with the two excised sections
absent in 100\% of cases; classification$\to$report finding agreement is 96\%
(cls$\to$report) and 92\% across the longer cls$\to$ASP$\to$report chain. The novel
region-identify capability answers with correctly echoed coordinates in 100\% of
sampled query boxes.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}

\textbf{Distance to the ceiling.} At family level the greedy $J$ reached 94\% of the
head's realised $J$; per-finding, mid-prevalence findings still leave 0.05--0.15 of $J$
on the table (nodules 0.43 vs.\ 0.52). Uniform $6{:}1$ was calibrated for the
prevalence mode, not per finding; the residual gap concentrates where prevalence or
score sharpness deviates from that mode. A per-finding weight schedule --- or, at
inference, per-finding log-prob thresholds read off the (unchanged) teacher-forced
margins --- would close most of it. \textbf{Where the trade bit.} High-prevalence
findings over-shot: cardiomegaly (spec 0.63) and consolidation (0.59) now over-assert;
their Youden still improved, but a deployment-facing configuration may prefer $4{:}1$
for these tags. \textbf{Regressions.} \texttt{low\_lung\_volumes} lost sensitivity
($-35\%$ $J$), plausibly a vocabulary-competition effect with sibling phrasing; and
\texttt{opacity} remains at $J{=}0$ for both stages because the model prefers specific
opacity terms over the umbrella token --- a lexical-mapping artefact of the silver
labels, not a perception failure. \textbf{Evaluation caveats.} Ground truth is an
LLM extraction of radiologist reports (silver, report-derived, with known prior-study
contamination); S2/S3 numbers are an interim 17{,}880-study cut of the running 136k
evaluation (the head row uses the completed full-set scoring); and the head's EER
thresholds were fit on the evaluation distribution, flattering the target slightly.
The full-set refresh will finalise all S2/S3 columns.

\section{Conclusion}

Stage-3 shows that for a jointly trained generative-and-discriminative VLM, the gap
between ``what the model knows'' (AUC of its scores) and ``what the model says''
(greedy assertions) is a \emph{controllable training variable}, not an inherent cost of
generation. Composing multiturn chains over frozen discriminative substrate and
re-anchoring the presence-token decision boundary with an asymmetric $6{:}1$ CE weight
moved the free-running operating point from the mute corner of the ROC curve
($J{=}0.28$, nodule family) to near the Youden optimum of the model's own scores
($J{=}0.52$ vs.\ ceiling $\approx$0.55), at a training cost of 11.7 GPU-node-hours and
with the classification head --- and therefore every head-based downstream consumer ---
untouched. Youden's index, being exactly the quantity that separates genuine gained
separation from threshold cosmetics, is the metric on which such interventions should
be designed and judged.
